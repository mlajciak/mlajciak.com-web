This is my personal list of selected articles, books… I am trying to select such that are not on the front page of every other must-read article.

### Non fiction

[Black Holes and Time Warps: Einstein's Outrageous Legacy by Kip Thorne](https://www.amazon.com/Black-Holes-Time-Warps-Outrageous/dp/0393312763)

### Fiction

[The Metamorphosis of Prime Intellect by Roger Williams](https://www.amazon.com/Metamorphosis-Prime-Intellect-Roger-Williams/dp/1411602196). (Not for weak minds)

[Nexus by Ramez Naam](https://www.amazon.com/Nexus-Trilogy-Book-1-ebook/dp/B00TOZI7FM). The scene, where Shu hacks Kade's mind and exposes her capabilities to him had is one of the best scence I have read so far. The book (I did not read 2 & 3 as of now) is in some parts more of C-tier action Holywood movie, but the near future sci-fi aspect is highly enjoyable and grounded.

[Dune: The Graphic Novel 1 & 2](https://www.amazon.com/DUNE-Graphic-Novel-Book-MuadDib/dp/1419749463). True page turner. Great for long flights and train rides.

[Dwarves by Markus Heitz](https://www.amazon.com/Dwarves-Markus-Heitz/dp/1841499641). Great alternative world for people (like me), who already read and studied the entire LOTR lore.
