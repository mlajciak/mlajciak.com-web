# AI Alignment

## Terms
*AI alignment* focuses on creating systems that reliably pursue objectives consistent with our (human) intentions, principles and values. Primary effort is to minimize the risks from goal misgeneralization or unintended optimization behaviors. Misgeneralization occurs, when a system learns well in training, but pursues a correlated but incorrect objective (proxy goal), that produce high reward during training (mostly RL). Thus AI system is generalized and can act in multiple environments, but internally it has wrong objective, which does not show up during training - high reward. This was famously demostrated in "Goal Misgeneralization in Deep Reinforcement Learning" by Langosco et al.
