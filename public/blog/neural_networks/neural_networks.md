# Neural Networks Explained

Neural networks are computational models inspired by biological neural networks. They form the foundation of modern deep learning.

## Key Concepts

- **Neurons**: Basic computational units that process inputs
- **Layers**: Organized groups of neurons
- **Weights**: Parameters that determine neuron behavior
- **Activation Functions**: Non-linear transformations

## Architecture

Neural networks typically consist of:

1. Input layer
2. Hidden layers
3. Output layer

## Training

Networks are trained using backpropagation and gradient descent to minimize loss functions.
