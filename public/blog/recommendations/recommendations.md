### Fiction

[The Metamorphosis of Prime Intellect by Roger Willimams](https://www.amazon.com/Metamorphosis-Prime-Intellect-Roger-Williams/dp/1411602196). (Not for weak minds)

[Nexus by Ramez Naam](https://www.amazon.com/Nexus-Trilogy-Book-1-ebook/dp/B00TOZI7FM). The scene, where Shu hacks Kade's mind and exposes her capabilities to him had "dropped my jaw" (I remember that I couldn't stop thinking about it for the rest of the day). The book (I did not read 2 & 3 as of now) is in some parts more of C-tier action Holywood movie, but the near future sci-fi aspect is great and grounded.

[Dune: The Graphic Novel 1 & 2](https://www.amazon.com/DUNE-Graphic-Novel-Book-MuadDib/dp/1419749463). True page turner. Great for long flights and train rides.
