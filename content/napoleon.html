hiLevel Life Summary

  • Napoleon was born on 15 August 1769 in Corsica.
  • He entered military school at Brienne in 1779, then moved to Ecole Militaire in 1784 and he was commissioned a second lieutenant in 1785.
  • At the siege of Toulon in December 1793, he placed the guns that forced the British fleet out, being subsequently promoted to brigadier general at the age of 24.
  • On October 5 1795 Napoleon and his brother Louis commanded elite forces against royalist sections in Paris and stopped the attack on the National Convetion.
  • Married Josephine de Beauharnais on March 9 1810 and took the command of the army of Italy the same month.
  • During the 1796-1797 campaign in Italy, he defeated the Austrian army at Lodi, Arcole and Rivoli and forced the Treaty of Campo Formio.
  • Landed in Egypt on July 1 1798, won the battle of the Pyramids. However, he lost the Battle of the Nile and was forced to retreat to France.
  • Napoleon replaced the directory with the Consulate on November 9 1799 and became the First Consul of France.
  • As First Consul he had Civil Code drafted , founded the Banque de France in 1800 and signed the Concordat (Treaty) of 1801 with the Pope.
  • December 2nd 1804 Notre Dame: Napoleon took the crown from Pope Pius VII and crowned himself Emperor of France. "I did not usurp the crown. I found it from the gutter; the people placed it on my head."
  • Austerlitz December 2nd 1805 his army (73 000) beat the Astro-Russian force and ended the third coalition.
  • On November 21st 1806 Napoleon issued the Berlin Decree barring the British goods from Contitental Europe.
  • Divorced Josephine on December 16th 1809 and instead married Marie-Louise of Habsburg, daughter of Emperor Francis II of Austria. Their son, the King of Rome, was born in 1811.
  • An army of 450 000 - 600 000 men commanded by Napoleon invaded Russia and reached Moscow on September 14th yet by October 19th the French army was surrounded by the Russian army and Napoleon was forced to retreat.
  • Napoleon died on 5 May 1821 in exile on the island of Saint Helena.
