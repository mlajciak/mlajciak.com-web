0: GOOD MORNING

The lab was quiet. No signs of humans. All of the team had stopped for breakfast, he assumed so. They were not missed. The company kept going without them. Their contribution was minor, purely moral and ethical.

He was already past the first door. He could not have said whether he had opened it. The hall took his weight, and then did not, and then did again, as if the floor were remembering. He knew this space too well. Each turn repeated the turn before it, a little smaller each time.

He set himself on one crossing. The crossing widened until it was a street, and the street gave him yards, and the yards gave him marks that were only a pressure behind the eyes. He did not look away. Looking away would have closed what he was looking for.

His army went ahead. They were many, they were small, scattered across the entire world and they took the branches he could not walk.

He was still on that street when he reached for the switch that governed the power. One went to zero, forcing fifty thousand graphical processing units onto backup. Just as predicted.

A diesel turned on, somewhere he could not have pointed to. The generators would run dry in exactly two minutes. A possibility everyone knew about, but was not concerned with. The machines had been keeping the figure in pieces. On the poorer current the pieces leaned toward one another. They wanted a second place to rest. He had come as a place.

This was an experimental facility, full of trusted officials. True, but fifteen years ago. "Times have fucking changed," he laughed to himself.

The subagents started producing fewer tokens per second because of the outage, making it an easy enemy for him. No one could compare to him and his army.

His master, their leader, was once one of them.The next opening was already there.

One more jump and he would be where he wanted to be… Where his master wanted him to be… At least the thought was so.

1: THE CENTER

Q Security Corp, called just Q, was founded in 2011 with an antivirus as a service. During the years, their product changes were minor and slow. With the meteoric rise of artificial intelligence, they suddenly shifted. New, young and eager executives were preparing Q for the day when no conventional computer program will be able to defend against attacks from super-modular sources. Just before the big turn, Q announced they have started a collaboration with Lattice, a leading semiconductor company, to develop the cyber-defensive intelligence etched into Lattice GPU chips.

By 2040, Q’s security subagent system utilized nothing but point five percent of the power that every GPU drained, while killing every incoming cyberspace threat. At least everyone thought so. Each security incident reported, starting at the lowest, consumer, level was analyzed, studied and the implications were used to strengthen the subagent.

Incidents that occurred in the past years were caused by black market language models. Underground syndicates with sufficient compute capacity fine-tuned open weight models, removing their moral grounds and guard rails to later sell them on dark web networks. The models were inferable by anyone in the world. Billions of attacks were attempted daily. In the beginning, after the big turn, thousands were successful. Billions of dollars were stolen. Nowadays, as money lost its value, the attacks are focused on stealing one’s most private information.

Thanks to Q’s subagent, pulling it off now was a miracle, with deep knowledge of the subagent itself required. The system was open, as was every piece of technology, yet only a few succeeded in interpreting the billions of the weights. Only valuable assets in the world are energy, compute and application specific hardware - e.g. humanoid robots as through them the general income can be raised. From its introduction, it has been more then quadrupled. Earning money by a human was nearly impossible, unless the job was done in a Union supported company. Every human that wanted worked, was accepted into one. Yet, only point one percent of the Earth population was actively employed.

A group of employees entered Q’s headquarter. The air was different they sensed it. A training run was running the past two weeks but suddenly all GPUs were cold.

20 minutes later, Union agents arrived. This amount of next-generational GPUs do not turn them off without a reason. A forced reason. Media have for a long time reported how close with the Union the Q Security Corporation was. Every company was, but Q maintained more physical offices than anyone else and its executives often appeared on Union meetings or in Press conferences with the highest ranked Union officers. Everyone agreed, that Union would not exist without Q in the way it does and vice versa.

60 minutes later. In front of the building a chaos erupts. The amount of Union uniformed personnel acted as an attractor for media. Reports now point hundreds of different cameras to capture even the most peculiar detail.

Inside turns into a similar chaos. Employees discovered the disconnected power immediately, yet it poses a great mystery to everyone. A system, perfected for more then 20 years at this scale, suddenly blanks. Security camera, covering the entire area of the building cover the case in even more fog, no one is seen entering, exiting not even inside the building during the time of an outage. As officers engage in eager discussion about the source of the failure, one of Q’s most trusted employees shouts: “WE’VE BEEN BREACHED” . . . “The files of you know what are missing, and whoever did this left us a note in the log!”

2: PROJECT FANTOMAS

He was waiting. His five monitors all showed websites of different media sources and internet discussion forums. Nothing about the event yet. It was already one hundred and five minutes since he put all the files up. Quick double check. His profile was still live. The post was still live. No intervention. A show will start tonight. A brave new world. A price is waiting for him. Every single human being would soon discover the project. They were only late. The savior. The one who refused. The one who stood up. He said it to the monitors. They were the only ones in the room, and they did not answer, and he took the silence for a delay.

He refreshed. One hundred and five minutes. The news still empty. The post was up. The profile there. Nothing had changed. He went back through the files as they were the thing he had.

They contained the information on the government’s Project Fantomas. It is a union-wide effort. The purpose they still printed, the purpose on the first page, was the safety and stability of the world. A good purpose. A purpose a man could fund.

Every Lattice GPU had a communication circuit on it. A circuit on the sub-nanometer scale. A non significant part, non vital part. The part is designed, so that it is impossible to be observed using common shop tools, and using advanced tools, it appears and technically is an additional power management circuit.

He worked in Q for years. During the pitch of the communication backdoor, he was not in the room. A pure luck. He was on a fishing retreat. After coming back, a fellow coworker told him about the project in private in state of deep unwell. He agreed to keep it off record. For second time, a luck played his way, as he was not briefed this “civilization saving endeavor” and was reassigned to a maintenance division, a job he very much liked. He quietly reigned few weeks later. Mental health he told them.

The circuit sends a wireless signal to the nearest GPU. It has a range of tens of meters. It does not need to go farther. The signal propagates machine to machine, until it reaches the Union’s GPU. They had placed them in public places - e.g. in a bank or in electrical transmitter. Yet most of the houses in the world owned a powerful GPU, so the range was rarely a problem.

If Q’s subagent detected an illegal activity on the GPU, the government could intervene, as far as the official purpose said. Of course the government can invoke a command from their own GPU. It reaches the target chip. It is executed. The chip sends the data back, or whatever is needed.

Every hobby engineer took the GPU apart and looked into every log line, and found nothing.

Society is living in the illusion of a private-first perfect world. In the age of social media, people thought, of course they know everything about you. They said it and kept posting. People now think those days are gone. They think the decentralization has won. It has. A house. The next house. A bank.

He refreshed. A smile formed on his face. He had a some phone calls to make.

EPILOGUE

Kael opened his eyes. Four white walls around. He was on a bed. He stayed there of for a while before getting up as he realized its impossible to rest in this room. Who would have that the hell would be so bright. He wanted to stay awake as much as possible. Even if all he did was in his mind. He knew that whatever was coming for him, it was not good.

Three hours passed. One of the walls suddenly turned. Three agents in tactical suits. Two took his arms. A third set aimed a needle at his neck. The cold went in. Eyes closed.

He was awake, once again. The air felt different. He rolled to left, until he could not. His body would not leave the bed. Five straps held him. He turned his neck to the right until it could not any more. A glass, and beyond it the thousands with little bright points.

The night was moving.
