About Me & Interests

I started programming at age 11 and built embedded systems and 3D-printed robotic platforms while studying Mechatronics at High School. Early work included reinforcement learning for control and hybrid multi-robot planning (RRT-APF with neural task allocation) on differential-drive platforms, published in Sensors 2025. My current focus is on world models. I am pursuing a BSc in Computer Science at Aarhus University.
