## About Me & Interests

I started programming at age 11 and built embedded systems plus 3D-printed robotic platforms while studying Mechatronics at Stredná priemyselná škola Dubnica nad Váhom. Early work included reinforcement learning for control and hybrid multi-robot planning (RRT-APF with neural task allocation) on differential-drive platforms, published in Sensors 2025. My current focus is on diffusion models for generative AI and their use in automated design and engineering pipelines. I am pursuing a BSc in Computer Science at Aarhus University.
